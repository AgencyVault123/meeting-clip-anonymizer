## 🎛️ HOW THE WEB UI WORKS:

1. **Open the URL** from your personal Mac browser
2. **Drag your cold call video** into the upload area
3. **Adjust voice slider if needed** (default 0.82 is perfect - natural deep voice)
4. **Click "Anonymize Video"**
5. **Wait ~1-2 minutes** (progress bar shows status)
6. **Download the anonymized video**
7. **Upload to YouTube**

**No folders. No command line. Just works.**
